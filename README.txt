POSTER STUDIO - SETUP

1. Extract the ZIP first.
2. Upload index.html and poster.png to the SAME folder on your hosting.
3. Open the page, choose your photo, adjust it, then download.
4. If opening index.html directly on your computer causes download issues, click Load poster image and choose poster.png from this folder.

No installation, external scripts, or backend required.
Source template was extracted from your PDF.
